## Download

**Windows 10/11:** download **`SteelBallAR-win-x64.zip`** below, unzip it, and double-click
**`SteelBallAR.exe`**. Your browser opens with the app. You don't need to install Node.js or anything else.

> Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the exe is not code-signed.
> Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

You don't want to download anything? Use the **Play in browser** link on the repository page.

## What's in this version

- Hand tracking with a spinning Steel Ball on your open palm: summon, spin, throw at the lens
- Three ball looks (new / classic / SBR-style) and three render styles (hybrid / anime / metal)
- Two-hand mode with a double throw
- Optional hat with head tracking and a fit panel
- Synthesised sound effects (bring your own sounds via `assets/audio/manifest.json`)
- Real-ball calibration, debug overlay, mirror and occlusion toggles

Everything runs locally in your browser. No video leaves your computer.
